Creator tool for music content

Jean Social Artist Autopilot

JSAA is an AI-assisted creator tool for preparing, reviewing and managing short-form music content for platforms such as TikTok and Instagram. The artist remains in control of every creative and publishing decision.

What JSAA does

  • Organizes songs, artwork and lyrics for social-content preparation.
  • Uses AI-assisted semantic analysis to understand song meaning and creative direction.
  • Generates visual concepts and supporting imagery for short-form music videos.
  • Prepares captions, hashtags and vertical video previews.
  • Requires explicit artist review and approval before content is prepared for publishing.

Creative workflow

Song → lyric understanding → visual concepts → artist selection → vertical video render → approval → publishing preparation

JSAA is designed to assist the creative process rather than replace the artist's judgment. Generated content can be accepted, edited, rejected or discarded.

Social-platform integration

JSAA may connect to supported social platforms through their official authorization and API systems. Account access is requested only for enabled features. Publishing is user-controlled and is not performed automatically without explicit user action.

Privacy and security

JSAA follows a local-first design. Project media, local databases and sensitive credentials are kept outside this public website. API keys, client secrets and access tokens are not published here.
